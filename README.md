# Customer-Segmentation-and-Retention-Analysis-Dashboard
📊 Customer Segmentation and Retention Analysis Dashboard
🎯 Objective
The objective of this project is to design an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides clear insights into customer segmentation, retention, and profitability. By leveraging RFM scores, CLV values, Recency analysis, and demographic filters, the dashboard highlights customer loyalty, churn risk, and value contribution in a business friendly format.
🛠 Tools & Technologies
•	Power BI → for dashboard creation and interactive visuals.
•	Power Query → for preprocessing and cleaning data
•	RFM & CLV metrics → for customer segmentation and profitability analysis
📊 Key Features
•	Summary KPIs
o	Total Customers: 50K
o	Total Revenue: 2.51bn
o	Average Spend: 50.27K
o	Active Customer %: 0.59
•	Customer Status Pie Chart
o	Active, Inactive, At Risk, Dormant distribution
•	Customer by Segment Bar Chart
o	Consumer, Premium, Small Business, Enterprise
•	Average CLV by RFM Score Bar Chart
o	Champions, Loyal, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, Need Attention
•	Customer Over Time Line Chart
o	Monthly trend showing customer growth and decline
•	Interactive Slicers
o	Gender filter (Male/Female)
o	Country filter (Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Spain, UAE, UK, USA)
✨ Insights
•	Champions and Loyal customers deliver the highest CLV, making them critical for retention strategies.
•	At Risk and Dormant segments show declining value, highlighting areas for re engagement.
•	Consumer segment dominates the customer base, while Enterprise remains the smallest but potentially high value.
•	Customer trend analysis reveals seasonal decline toward December, signaling churn risk.
•	Slicers enable quick drill downs by gender and geography to uncover demographic patterns.
📘 Outcomes
•	A comprehensive dashboard that connects customer behavior (RFM, Recency) with profitability (CLV).
•	Clear visualization of customer segments, activity status, and value contribution.
•	A strategic tool for decision making in customer retention, churn management, and growth planning.
